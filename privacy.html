Privacy Policy — Mobile App

Last updated: April 2026
This Privacy Policy applies to the Kakkamvelly Sreekrishna Temple Android app (package: page.kakkamvellytemple.app). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect or sell any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

The Kakkamvelly Sreekrishna Temple app does not collect any personally identifiable information.

The following non-personal data may be collected automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage data (screens viewed, app opens). No personal information is included.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — a device token is generated to deliver push notifications. This token is not linked to your identity.
  • Weather data — the app fetches current weather for Nadapuram, Kerala using the Open-Meteo public API. No location permission is required or requested.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Name, email address, or phone number
  • ❌ Device location or GPS data
  • ❌ Photos, contacts, or files from your device
  • ❌ Payment or financial information
  • ❌ Login credentials or account information
  • ❌ Any biometric data

3. Push Notifications

The app may send optional push notifications about:

  • 🎊 Upcoming temple festivals (Vishu, Janmashtami, Onam, etc.)
  • 🍛 Monthly Annadhanam reminders (first Sunday)
  • 🛕 Special temple announcements

You can disable notifications at any time from your Android device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics + Cloud Messaging) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Open-Meteo API — free weather data, no API key or personal data required — Open-Meteo Terms

We do not share any data with advertisers or other third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is a spiritual/religious app suitable for all ages. It does not collect any information from children under 13. No account creation is required to use the app.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no user data stored on our servers. The app operates primarily in read-only mode — displaying temple information, schedules, and live weather.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
